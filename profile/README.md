# Strong Towns Community Developers & Designers

Open source tools for advocates who do the math. Value-per-acre maps, parking audits, housing trackers and more, built by local groups and shared so any town can use them.

- **Website:** [strongtowns.dev](https://strongtowns.dev)
- **Discord:** [discord.gg/r6erzTuEmn](https://discord.gg/r6erzTuEmn)

## Featured projects

### [Pracre](https://github.com/strongtowns/Pracre)
A desktop app that turns the parcel and assessment files your town publishes into 2D and 3D maps of value and tax revenue per acre. Runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

[Download](https://github.com/strongtowns/Pracre/releases/latest) · [Quick start](https://github.com/strongtowns/Pracre#quick-start)

### [Urbanism YouTube](https://github.com/strongtowns/UrbanismYoutube)
Every new video from Strong Towns and other urbanism YouTube channels, newest first, with an RSS feed and Discord notifications.

[Open Urbanism YouTube](https://strongtowns.dev/urbanismyoutube/)

## More projects
- [ValuePerAcre2](https://github.com/strongtowns/ValuePerAcre2) maps tax productivity parcel by parcel
- [ParkingLots](https://github.com/strongtowns/ParkingLots) maps every surface parking lot in an area from OpenStreetMap data
- [ssmuhplanner](https://github.com/strongtowns/ssmuhplanner) checks a lot for small-scale multi-unit housing and lays out buildings against a BC zoning bylaw
- [ScrollingComparison](https://github.com/strongtowns/ScrollingComparison) makes scrolling before and after videos of roads and neighbourhoods

Built a tool for your own local conversation? Bring it to the Discord.
